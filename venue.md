The European Winter School on MLIR 2027 will take place at **[Big Sky](https://www.bigsky-hotel.com/en)** in [Chamonix-Mont-Blanc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamonix), France.

**Stay package:** Monday 4 January noon (included) → Friday 8 January noon (excluded).  
People who wish to arrive on Sunday can arrange an **extra night** directly with the hotel (outside the school package).

**Note:** Big Sky is about **5.5 km** from the **Chamonix SNCF train station**. Plan your arrival accordingly.
{: .notice--warning}

The hotel provides a **free shuttle**. More information will be given at registration.

# Getting Here

- **Train:** Chamonix-Mont-Blanc SNCF station (then ~5.5 km to the hotel — use the hotel shuttle or a taxi).
- **Plane:** Geneva airport (~1 h by car/shuttle); Lyon airport (~2 h by car).
- **Car:** free parking at the hotel; Geneva ~1 h, Lyon ~2 h, Paris ~6 h.
